## Assessment Methodologies
### Information Gathering (Reconnaissance)
- Also refer to [[Information Gathering - Hack the Box Academy]]
#### Introduction to Information Gathering
- First phase of penetration test
- Collect information on individual, employee, client, supplier, contractors, company, website, IP addresses, hostnames, systems, technologies
	- People Targets
		- Company
			- Name, prior names, history, employees, suppliers, clients, contractors
		- Employees
			- Full name, email address, phone number, job position, job function, colleagues, years in company, prior company, social media posts (Facebook, Twitter, LinkedIn)
		- Supplier
			- Supplier contact information (name, email, phone), company name, recent supplier communications, purchase history (product or service), reviews
		- Client
			- Client contact information (name, email, phone), number of clients, recent client communications, purchase history (product or service), reviews
		- Contractors
	- Technology Targets
		- Website
		- IP address
		- Hostname
		- System
- Target Scoping
	- Process of defining **exactly** what systems, networks, or applications **allowed** to test.
	- Answers the question: **What am I allowed to ==collect information== about?**
	- Common Types of Targets:
		- Domain-based Targets
			- Primary domain and subdomains
			- E.g. example.com, testsite.local, mail.example.com, admin.example.com, dev.example.com)
